Safe Space and Usage Guidelines

1. Maintain Clearance

Leave a minimum margin around the logo equal to 0.25 times its height.

2. No Distortion

Do not stretch, compress, or alter the logo's proportions.

3. Color Usage

Do not use unauthorized color schemes or display the logo against a background that clashes with its colors.

Typography and Layout

Primary Font: Inter / System Sans-Serif

For interfaces and body text

Parker — Precision Parking · Smart Navigation for the Future

Heading Recommendations: Weight 700-900, Line Height 1.02, Slightly Tight Kerning.

Use medium weight for light/small fonts to enhance readability.

Body Text: Weight 400-500, Line Height 1.6, Normal Kerning.
